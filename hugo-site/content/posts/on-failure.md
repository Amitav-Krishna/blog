I'm not failing enough.  Today I went to the gym, and I was doing my bench press, when a fellow walked over and corrected my grip; my grip was narrow, but to workout one's chest one must use a moderate grip.  I was thinking on the bicycle ride that that's the first time in a few weeks I've really failed at something.  I don't think that's a good thing.  The times in my life I got the most done were the times when I was failing a lot, and the lack of failure is indicative that I'm not trying enough things.  One thing I'm going to add to these posts going forward is a failure from the day.
